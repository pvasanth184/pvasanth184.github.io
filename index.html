About

Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of Maryland, College Park, under the supervision of Prof. Yanir A. Rubinstein. I expect to graduate in May 2027, and am currently on the job market.

I am broadly interested in geometric analysis, and study problems at the interface of potential theory, a priori estimates, and differential and symplectic geometry. Recently I have been exploring how geometric methods can inform research in and applications of AI

Internships

Quantum Computing Research Intern at Error Corp, June 2025 -- August 2025.
Modelled the problem of minimizing first order interaction/cross-talk errors in a 2-qubit quantum system as the differential geometry problem of identifying optimal control curves in the lie group SU(2). Implemented a numerical solver in Python (JAX) to optimize control pulses, achieving a significant reduction in error rates.
